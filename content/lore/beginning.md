# THE BEGINNING OF THE END

In the time before memory, **Arceus** stood as the architect of all creation, the Original One who shaped reality itself. But perfection breeds contempt, and in the infinite expanse of his power, he grew to despise what it had built. *The world was too complete, too ordered, too stable.*

*Where was the artistry in eternal completion? Where was the thrill of watching intricate designs collapse into entropy?*

And so, the Original One began to plot the unmaking of everything he had created. He did not destroy the world outright, that would be too simple, too merciful. Instead, he reached into the deepest foundations of reality, the **Void Between Worlds**, and tore open a wound.

From the Void, he pulled forth corrupted versions of his own creations: **The Administrator**, what remained of Palkia after the Void consumed it, **The Broken End**, Dialga twisted beyond recognition, and most terrible of all, **Integrity**, Giratina reborn as the Herald of Unmaking. 
Under Arceus's influence, Integrity became a living manifestation of corruption that spread like a disease through reality itself.

*Where Integrity walked, the world broke, physics failed, memories shattered and souls began to splinter.*
