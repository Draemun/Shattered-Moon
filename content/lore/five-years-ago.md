During an expedition, Null discovered something that should have never been there.
**Void Echoes**, they called them, crystalline structures that emitted sounds that shouldn't exist. They warped space, **made Pokémon vanish when they drew too close**, and felt...wrong, in ways no one could explain. 
*They didn't know they were fragments of the Void itself, leaking into reality.*

And for a while, nothing seemed to happen, until the Zoroark was ambushed by The Broken End. Across **126 different timelines**, it hunted and killed him, each death fracturing his consciousness further. The primary shard became **Null**, a ghostly presence still trying to warn and help others, but the other 126 fragments became **Nihil**, twisted echoes that wear Null's face and serve the Void directly.

Sekani found a **Soothe Bell** that wouldn't stop ringing. The sound was wrong, vibrating in frequencies that bent reality. Over time, his body warped into something unnatural. He became **Curved**, an aggressive entity that appears in tunnels and caves. **Those who encounter him never come back.**

Blackout ventured deep into a cave one day, following some voices asking for help and **never returned**. His teammates found only scorch marks that spiraled in impossible directions, claw marks that seemed to fold through rocky walls and terrain.

Kyria was the last of the first to vanish.
She experienced the **Moon Glitch**, a phenomenon where Arceus's corruption manifested physically in the world. One night, the moon itself seemed to crack and bleed void-energy across the sky. 
*That same night, Integrity materialized in Kyria's home; she tried to run and to fight...But you cannot fight the fundamental breakdown of existence.* 
Records become unclear here. Some survivors claim they saw Kyria flee somehow, others say she became something else entirely.
