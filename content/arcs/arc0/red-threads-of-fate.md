Sylvia Solreeder, a splintered espeon had set her sights to siege the capital of Last Haven. She and her lackeys of Magnemite set their sights on three important locations to seek and destroy. Up above her soars a splintered Alpha Dragapult loaded with a high-yield bomb and several dreepy's to shoot down. Damage to important structures such as the Guild, Hospital, and Radio Towers.

In response, a group of pokemon gathered to confront and stop her which includes the following:

```
Caesar the Leafeon
Silas the Scout Echelon Jolteon and her lover
Kame the Godborne Wartortle
Nimmo the irritable Marshbite 
Demo the Builder Echelon Drifloon
```

During their battle, Sylvia utilized the Magnemites targeting parameters by commanding them to use Lock-On on one of her three main targets. The group quickly discovered that it was allowing the Alpha Dragpult to launch its payload of weaponry. Demo goes to surround the field in aerial terrain to weaken the numerous threats and allow himself to ascend and meet up with the alpha splintered. Caesar had managed to defeat one of the Magnemites with a precise blow to it with a leaf blade.

While doing battle and firmly holding herself against these five pokemon, two Magnemite and a mysterious Sableye co-ordinated their efforts with her. A couple of turns later, a spite was casted reducing her trump card PP to just one while ion deluge and metal sound was used. Sylvia launches her signature move towards the turtle. An electric boosted, 200 Base Power move that can't miss sound has likely killed Kame. However, when it did end his life for a moment, he returned to the realm of the living through Arceus' blessing.

Her efforts were beginning to falter and fail as soon as more direct blows were returned to Sylvia and the Magnemites. Demo had recently damaged the outer shell of the bomb with a burst of lightning followed by igniting it with fire. A chain reaction ensued, the bomb held on its chest exploding and Demo back towards the ground.

Sylvia would make her last stand as her primary weapon was destroyed. Kame manages to disarm her by speaking through one of the magnemites connected through her helmet while Silas in his blistering speed had managed to nail her with a precise blow of his katana. Meanwhile, Caesar had managed to recklessly intercept one of the dreepy from hitting the guild, almost costing him his life. Three remaining magnemites fuse together to evolve, but not before Kame jumps over it and gets his leg caught. Sylvia urged them to flee from battle before she succumbs to her injuries and ultimately surrenders. Last haven was spared from her wrath and still wonders if there's hope for her to one day break the cycle of Arceus' influence someday...
