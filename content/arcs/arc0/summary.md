The moon cracked open and revealed a red eye at its core.
Soul Splinters massed at Last Haven's barrier while Palkia arrived behind them, piloted by Arceus. Kailani damaged the Echo Spire, weakening the barrier.

Null, one of the first splinters, appeared and gave intel about 'chains' that Arceus was using to control the splinters, freeing some to fight on the guild's side, before Palkia erased him with Space Render.

A Mega Golurk and a Mega Tyranitar were brought down through sustained effort from the full field; with the anchor points gone, the horde lost cohesion. A mysterious Fire Pledge appeared from an unknown source, Darwin combined Grass Pledge with it, and the accumulated damage pushed Palkia back.

A dark tendril from the sky dragged him away before he could retaliate. Arceus had grown bored and the moon stitched itself closed.
