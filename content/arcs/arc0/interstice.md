Several pokemon find themselves in a displaced space, where multiple realities have bled into each other and time flows in a non-linear pattern. Stone shatters like glass, water flows upward against gravity, fire burns without leaving behind ashes, and there are cracks in the fabric of reality that can be seen but not touched. In this displaced space, they experience memories of events that have yet to happen, knowledge of events that will never come to be, and temptations of power that can be.

Agni finds a magnificent sword that she crafted in a different reality, but it was never hers to own. By mustering the courage to melt the sword down and unravel it completely, she accepts that what was never crafted by her cannot be allowed to remain.

Attakario experiences memories of different worlds and timelines bleeding into his mind: some enticing, some terrifying. He was born without a leg, but opens his eyes to find himself with a perfectly intact left leg. By accepting that no part of himself was ever missing and that having only three limbs is who he is, he rejects the gift of making him "whole".

Xerneas is faced with the question of what would have happened if she was the one splintered instead of Yveltal. Life and death are two sides of the same coin, and there cannot be one without the other. She finds herself with the power to wield both life and death.
