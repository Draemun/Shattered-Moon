A lullaby no one recognized pulled a scattered group through endlessly looping residential streets. Every path circled back to the same crossroads except one.

The streets funneled into a foggy pentagonal plaza. Gary spotted collapsed bodies through the mist and pushed in; the singing stopped.

At the end of the street they found a splintered Meloetta flanked by a Musharna and an Audino, standing over several pokemon they had been slowly putting to sleep. The Meloetta hit Gary and Crowea immediately with Relic Song.

Kailani turned on the Musharna and drove into it at full speed. Crowea woke up angry and redirected herself to the Meloetta. Gary followed with Moonblast. The Meloetta was already staggering when a wounded Talonflame crawled out of the fog behind it and put it down with Temper Flare.

Her name was Ainar, one of the best blacksmiths in the region. She had been in the area for materials when the fog came and had managed to evacuate a few residents before the Meloetta started dropping the rest. One of the sleeping pokemon on the ground was her apprentice on her first solo delivery.

An Arcanine among the fallen did not wake up.

Gary healed who he could and teleported away without a word. The fog lifted as the fight ended and they moved everyone out before it came back.
