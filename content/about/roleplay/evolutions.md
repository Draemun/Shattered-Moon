# EVOLUTION METHODS

### Standard Level-Up Evolution

- Participate in any combat RP that awards stats
- You may evolve immediately after earning stats, or save it for later

### Evolution Items

- Purchase from the server shop
- Earn as mission rewards

| Item Type | Examples |
|---|---|
| Evolution Stones | Fire, Water, Thunder, Leaf, Moon, Sun, Shiny, Dusk, Dawn, Ice |
| Trade Items | Link Cable |
| Specialty Items | Prism Scale, Protector, Electirizer, Magmarizer, etc. |

### Friendship Evolution

- Form a meaningful friendship with another OC or NPC in RP
- Left to personal judgment. Must be reasonable and demonstrated through RP
- No specific number of RPs required

### Time-Based Evolution

- Set your RP during the appropriate time of day
- Must earn stats during the RP

### Move-Based Evolution

| Type | Requirement |
|---|---|
| Single Use | Know the move and use it while gaining stats |
| Multiple Uses | Use the move a specific number of times (e.g., 10 times) |

# FORM CHANGE MECHANICS

### Cannot Change Mid-Battle

| Pokémon | Requirements |
|---|---|
| Rotom | Purchase Rotom Catalog / Choose form before battle / Locked signature move |
| Silvally | Purchase Memory item / Choose Memory before battle / Multi-Attack changes following type |
| Deoxys | Own Meteorite (purchasable or gimmick) / Choose form before battle / 1.5x stat boost on entry depending on form |
| Shaymin | Own Gracidea Flower (Can be purchased) / Choose form before battle / No auto-revert |
| Oricorio | Purchase Nectar / Choose form via profile update / Locked during battle |
| Genesect | Purchase Drive / Choose Drive before battle / Cannot switch mid-combat |

### Can Change Mid-Battle

| Pokémon | Mechanics |
|---|---|
| Castform | Changes form with weather (Rain, Sun, Hail/Snow) |
| Cherrim | Sunshine Form activates in sunny weather, reverts otherwise |
| Keldeo | Changes to Resolute Form when using Secret Sword |
| Meloetta | Relic Song toggles between Aria and Pirouette forms |
| Darmanitan | Zen Mode triggers at low HP, reverts when healed |
| Aegislash | King's Shield toggles between Shield and Blade forms |
| Morpeko | Alternates form every comment (Full Belly <-> Hungry) |
| Mimikyu | Disguise absorbs one hit, breaks after damage (Z-Moves bypass it) |
| Eiscue | Ice Face absorbs one physical hit, reforms in Hail/Snow |
| Minior | Shields Down triggers at low HP, reverts when healed |
| Wishiwashi | School Form at high HP, Solo Form at low HP, can reform |
| Palafin | Flip Turn activates Hero Form, reverts at low HP |
| Sawsbuck & Deerling | Form changes based on RP environment |

- Always update your character profile after evolution
- Be reasonable and communicate with RP partners about form changes
- Some form changes are purely aesthetic (like Sawsbuck), others are mechanical (like Aegislash)
