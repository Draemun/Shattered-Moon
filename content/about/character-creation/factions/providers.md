# THE PROVIDERS (Resources & Healing)

**Min Age:** 16 years

| Sub-Group | Role |
|---|---|
| Cultivators | Grow berries and herbs in protected gardens |
| Healers | Treat injuries and illness |
| Quartermasters | Manage supply inventory and rationing |
| Traders | Negotiate with wandering merchants and other settlements |

### Responsibilities

- Manage food stores and rations
- Cultivate what little grows in safe zones
- Prepare medicines and healing supplies
- Coordinate supply distribution
- Trade with other settlements
