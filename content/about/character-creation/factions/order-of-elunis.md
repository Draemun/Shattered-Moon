# THE ORDER OF ELUNIS

For as long as the Rings of Light has been seen in Miralen, the **Order of Elunis** has stood stalwartly by its side. It is composed of devout paladins and priests, and it is a holy order of crusaders who wield their might in the name of Arceus. The Light of Arceus is their guiding beacon and the source of salvation for monkind; it is their sworn duty to spread His Light and save lost souls who have sinned and turned away from the path of radiance. In particular, **Darkrai** are viewed as wicked sinners, demons of shadow who must be purged to rid the world of nightmares and grief.

The order was founded by a **Cresselia** with the family name of **Elunis**, and since its founding, the order has been led by a Cresselia of their founder's bloodline. Over the centuries, marriages with mon of different species have resulted in various hybrids, but the tradition of a Cresselia of the Elunis name leading the holy order has held strong to this day. The order is currently led by **Delonne Elunis**, a Cresselia who is well-respected in her own right.

When the Shattering occurred and the Rings of Light fell divided, **Delonne Elunis** approached **Wilfred Mayworth** and struck a deal with him. She would alter the valorous teachings of the Elunis in subtle and unnoticeable ways to turn the order into devout soldiers whose goals would align with the Mayworth sect. In return, the Order of Elunis would retain its noble standing even as the world fell to pieces, and the faithful members of the order would be spared from splintering. Tenets that praised Arceus' righteous light turned into a depiction of the armageddon as His divine trial, from which the worthy would be chosen. Oaths of following the path of radiance and honour turned into oaths of seeking willing martyrs who would become Arceus' eyes and bear the crucifix of His Light. Rather than being hunted, they are captured to be splintered and turned into **Thorn Weavers** as a form of *"purification"* by forcing Arceus' holy light into them. *Only the strongest will retain their mind after the ritual*, and those are given the highest honour of joining their ranks. Those whose minds shattered are killed on sight.

Delonne was crafty with her words, always mixing truth into lies. To maintain the reputation of the Elunis in the public eye, she made sure to have the order seen aiding the public with great fanfare. They often travel across Miralen to offer healing to the sick and injured, or to defend villages against foes they cannot handle. They also take those who are willing to be splintered into their protection and treat them as honoured guests. As a result, they are seen as valiant crusaders who mercilessly defend the innocent and protect the splintered who are shunned from society.
