# THE VOID HUNTERS (Offence & Entity Handling)

**Min Age:** 20 years

| Sub-Group | Role |
|---|---|
| Strike Teams | Target and eliminate specific entities |
| Zone Clearers | Purge corruption from designated areas |
| Tacticians | Develop combat strategies per entity type |
| Artificers | Craft and maintain specialized gear and weaponry |

### Responsibilities

- Actively hunt and eliminate entities
- Clear corruption from recoverable zones
- Develop entity-specific combat tactics
- Acquire materials for unbreakable weapons
- Test experimental weapons and strategies
