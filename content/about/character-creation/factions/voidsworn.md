It is not known when the first organized religions started appearing across Miralen. However, historical records show **The Rings of Light** as one of the first major institutions. They vehemently worshiped **Arceus**, believing him to be the creator of Miralen. At first, records show they were a nomadic clan. They traveled around Miralen during the early harmony age, preaching their beliefs to all who would listen. They were advocates for peace and abhorred the consumption of other mons; a practice that was still common at the time. Records record them traveling from what is the modern day **Lake of Memories** all the way to the east coast in an area that is now known as **Keshari's Tail**.

The Rings of Light settled into the area, marking its transition from a nomadic clan to starting its Keshari roots. They were led by the family **Maeuurth**, who became the first to keep organized records of the religious activities.

These records give a deeper look into the rituals and beliefs of the older religion. The fundamental traditions worship Arceus as their creator. Of course, Arceus not being on Miralen, the cult was given an object to revere in his stead. Throughout the millennia this object was referred to as **'The Relic'**; an extension of Acerus' good will towards the Rings of Light. The Relic has been used in many different rituals and traditions within the religion.

Despite the common trend of older religious groups failing after some years, the Rings of Light miraculously managed to stay intact. As the shift from the Harmony age to the New Age began to progress, their core message started to change too. Of course, Arceus was still the center of it all, but the surrounding traditions had morphed. Their message became less about the love of His mons and more about his wrath for those who did not believe. As the generations of leaders came and went, the rules within the compound got stricter.

The last Mon to lead the Rings of Light was a mon by the name of **Wilfred Mayworth**. He ran it just as his forefathers did, preaching Arceus' word and keeping his followers in line. That was until **The Shattering** destroyed Miralen.

The Religion didn't have an explanation for it. Why would Arceus turn on his beloved creations? Why would he destroy everything he'd built? The religion split; those who left, those who doubted, and those who still believed. Infighting within the religion was at an all time high. The religion needed a way to explain the actions of the Creator it had believed in for thousands of years.

*Arceus; a wrathful God, but not a cruel one. His trial had finally arrived to test the will of His followers. Those who are splintered, but not outright hostile, are considered blessed by Him. They have strong enough belief and willpower to become one of his vessels, whom he can work through. Once only Arceus' most loyal followers remain as splinters will they be saved, taken off to paradise.*

At least, that is what Wilfred proclaimed.

His most loyal followers continued to preach Arceus' word as the hasty transition from The Rings of Light to **the Voidsworn** began.

*The Void is not darkness, for to be in darkness is to not believe in his light. No, the Void is the lack of darkness as it is the lack of light. The Void is the absence of temptation, the absence of worldly beliefs, and the absence of everything. The Void leaves nothing but room for Arceus' will. Through the Void, Arceus will restore all his believers to paradise.*

**The Voidsworn will continue their mission at all costs.**

Life within the Voidsworn revolves around Arceus. Short morning sermons are given within the compound, while Sundays are spent partaking in longer services and rituals.

The Voidsworn are tight; both in community and rules. The rules are strict, but necessary. A true follower will not falter due to the ways of the world. Morning sermons are mandatory and prayers happen before every meal. **Sunday** is the most important day of the week as this is when followers receive **The Light Blessing**. During this weekly ritual, **Sunny Day** is used to call down His light, cleansing the followers of their sins if they confess to Arceus their wrongdoings. It is one of the most important traditions within the religion. Those that miss a cleansing are said to be burdened by their sins until next time they are able to confess. Other rituals happen more rarely on a case-by-case basis. *What matters above all else is adherence to the community rules and true belief in Him.* Adherence to ones daily schedule and the rules is expected and tracked. Minor violations earn correction in the form of extra chores, extra prayers with an elder, and the expectation of intense repentance. Repeat offenders are ostracized from the community and their social standing takes a hit; affecting not just themselves, but their close family and friends too. Serious violations or a large accumulation of smaller infractions earn **expulsion** from the group altogether. Arceus loves all, but a repeat offender cannot be kept around for they can cause others to stumble. They'll have to find their own way back to His light.

For more significant rituals and festivities, the Relic is used.

The initiation of new members is a long winded ceremony where those looking to convert profess their faith in front of the congregation. When all have made their statement, The Relic is placed upon their forehead and a prayer is recited. It is a tradition that has lived on since the times of the Rings of Light. It is simply referred to as the **Initiation Ceremony**.

The Blessing of Splinters, known as the **Voidsheart Ceremony**, is a newer ceremony that has become fundamental to the Voidsworn's practice. Those who have been splintered prepare flowery, romanticized stories about the splintering process. Emphasis is put on describing how Arceus is going to work through them to help convert others. These speeches are usually recited on Sunday mornings when the sun is first rising. Afterwards, the community partakes in a small festival where local flowers are picked and worn by all for the day.

The community has a few festivals celebrating holidays each year. **Creation Day** is observed the first of every year. The community comes together and hosts a dinner for all; even non religious members; to partake in. Smaller children make crafts showing Miralen or the sun, while older children decorate the compound in glassworks and crystals. While the true creation date of Miralen is of course not known, the First marks new beginnings for all.

**Virdan's Bounty** is observed in the springtime when the flowers begin to bloom. Named after the locals Virdan lake, it is a day to appreciate the land Arceus has blessed the Voidsworn with. It also marks the beginning of planting season, something that the Voidsworn take very seriously. Community members write small notes about their future hopes, along with their thankfulness to Arceus. A piece of twine is strung through the notes and hung on trees within the compound. While not a part of the traditional festivities, some families will give small gifts to their closest loves.

**Reunion** is a holiday which has been celebrated for as long as the Rings of Light have been settled. For one week during the year, all the traveling priests return to the Voidsworn compound to report on the success of their missions. The first day is composed of large gatherings with lots of food and many different groups singing traditional hymns in the elder language. Travelers are welcome home by family and friends. The next four days of the holiday are mostly composed of different aforementioned rituals, reports from the priests, and singing. The final day is spent simply resting; it is the one day of the year most of the compound is left to relax, allowing family members one last day together before traveling members set off once more.

*Of course, not all is what it seems.* The religion prides itself on looks, especially to outsiders. Those in charge manipulate the congregation in any way they can to keep the masses dormant.

The **Relic**, said to be an artifact from Arceus himself, is a dupe. The one used in ceremony is expertly carved from a **Fire Gem**, looking near indistinguishable from the real Relic.
The real Relic is used to subdue **Koraidon**. In reality, it is a fragment of the **Red Chain**; those who touch the chain unprotected have their energy sapped away, risking killing them. Koraidon's awareness has been fragmented and dulled due to the Chain. As long as strong light is available, Koraidon generates energy passively. The cult keeps a constant stream of sunlight through the use of sunny day and reflection to the chamber where Koraidon is kept. The energy Koraidon produces is siphoned and used to sustain Voidsworn infrastructure.

*There are no existing records of Koraidon's capture.* Whether they've been lost or deliberately destroyed is up for debate, but no one has the power nor suspicion to investigate the Voidsworn.

Only the leader, **Wilfred**, and his second in command, **Pecharunt**, know this truth. *A third knows about the Red Chain, but he knows better than to speak out about it.*

Splintered souls who aren't violent are treated as royalty in the community, but not all splintering goes well. Many are left violent. These mons end up kidnapped, thrown into an elaborate underground dungeon of sorts.
*Those without memories can be taught His will. Those who choose to fight simply need reeducated.* It's a simple system, one that the Voidsworn has found to work quite well.

---

The compound is split into a strict hierarchy. *Respect to your superiors is taken seriously, even if they may be younger or less experienced.*

The **Mayworth** family is at the top of that totem pole; **Wilfred** runs the compound while his two daughters are treated like royalty. His heir will return to the religion, one way or another. Bloodshed or not.
**Pecharunt** is treated on the same level as the Mayworth. They too, are treated as royalty.

**The High Rings** is a group of elders made up of those from both the Voidsworn and the Order of Elunis. They know many of the secrets the cult keeps behind closed doors and ensures those doors remain closed. They're tasked with making sure the other members stay happy and obedient, and the feisty splinters get reeducated.

Those within the **Order of Elunis** are considered higher than most. They are extremely devout individuals who go out to spread His word. They do not know of the horrors that dwell beneath their feet and it is expected to stay that way. Members of the Order are considered honorable mons who others should look up to.

Splinters who are devout to the cause are treated well. They're the physical manifestation of all the Voidsworn believes in, making them important to the cause. For the most part, they live as other members do; occasionally they are tasked with helping convince others to convert.

The majority of the cult is made up of average members whose family has been in the religion for generations. They work hard to keep the whole compound running while wholeheartedly believing in the Voidsworn's message.

*For the Voidsworn, most secrets are best kept as secrets.* They have their way of life and stick strongly to it. As the world gets more desperate, it's simply a matter of time before they start spreading.

### The Order of Elunis

For as long as the Rings of Light has been seen in Miralen, the **Order of Elunis** has stood stalwartly by its side. It is composed of devout paladins and priests, and it is a holy order of crusaders who wield their might in the name of Arceus. The Light of Arceus is their guiding beacon and the source of salvation for monkind; it is their sworn duty to spread His Light and save lost souls who have sinned and turned away from the path of radiance. In particular, **Darkrai** are viewed as wicked sinners, demons of shadow who must be purged to rid the world of nightmares and grief.

The order was founded by a **Cresselia** with the family name of **Elunis**, and since its founding, the order has been led by a Cresselia of their founder's bloodline. Over the centuries, marriages with mon of different species have resulted in various hybrids, but the tradition of a Cresselia of the Elunis name leading the holy order has held strong to this day. The order is currently led by **Delonne Elunis**, a Cresselia who is well-respected in her own right.

When the Shattering occurred and the Rings of Light fell divided, **Delonne Elunis** approached **Wilfred Mayworth** and struck a deal with him. She would alter the valorous teachings of the Elunis in subtle and unnoticeable ways to turn the order into devout soldiers whose goals would align with the Mayworth sect. In return, the Order of Elunis would retain its noble standing even as the world fell to pieces, and the faithful members of the order would be spared from splintering. Tenets that praised Arceus' righteous light turned into a depiction of the armageddon as His divine trial, from which the worthy would be chosen. Oaths of following the path of radiance and honour turned into oaths of seeking willing martyrs who would become Arceus' eyes and bear the crucifix of His Light. Rather than being hunted, they are captured to be splintered and turned into **Thorn Weavers** as a form of *"purification"* by forcing Arceus' holy light into them. *Only the strongest will retain their mind after the ritual*, and those are given the highest honour of joining their ranks. Those whose minds shattered are killed on sight.

Delonne was crafty with her words, always mixing truth into lies. To maintain the reputation of the Elunis in the public eye, she made sure to have the order seen aiding the public with great fanfare. They often travel across Miralen to offer healing to the sick and injured, or to defend villages against foes they cannot handle. They also take those who are willing to be splintered into their protection and treat them as honoured guests. As a result, they are seen as valiant crusaders who mercilessly defend the innocent and protect the splintered who are shunned from society.
