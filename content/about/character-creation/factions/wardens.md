# THE WARDENS (Defense & Security)

**Min Age:** 20 years

| Sub-Group | Role |
|---|---|
| Watchtower Guard | Stationed on second-floor balconies, 24/7 observation |
| Patrol Units | Regular patrols around safe zone boundary |
| Rapid Response | Elite team handling entity encounters |
| Trainers | Teach survival and combat techniques to refugees |

### Responsibilities

- Guard Guild perimeter and watch posts
- Patrol safe zones
- Respond to entity sightings and incursions
- Train members
- Lead evacuation protocols when necessary
