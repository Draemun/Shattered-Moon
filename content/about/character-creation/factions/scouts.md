# THE SCOUTS (Reconnaissance & Supply)

**Min Age:** 18 years

| Sub-Group | Role |
|---|---|
| Sky Scouts | Aerial reconnaissance, fastest information delivery |
| Ground Expeditions | Gather supplies from abandoned settlements |
| Route Markers | Establish and maintain safe paths |
| Rescuer | Extract refugees from dangerous zones |

### Responsibilities

- Map safe routes between settlements
- Locate resources and supplies
- Identify corruption hotspots and entity territories
- Establish and maintain supply lines
- Guide refugees to the Guild
