# STARTER REWARDS

After receiving staff approval, you may head to "team bag" to claim your starter rewards:

- 10x Oran Berries
- 5x Reviver Seeds
- 2x Escape Orbs
- Guild Map (Shows you safe areas and nearby Waystones)
- Partner's Promise
  - Grants a temporary (3 turns) omniboost boost if your partner falls (because of a foe) in battle
  - Does not stack if multiple teammates fall
