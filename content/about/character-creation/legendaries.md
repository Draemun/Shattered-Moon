Legendaries are anomalies created by Arceus long before time itself began. They were the first specimens to walk Miralen.

There is only one of each, with no descendants. They were designed to be aromantic and asexual, complete unto themselves.

To most Pokémon, they were myths, distant and unknowable.

Arceus considers them his highest priority.

He wants to break them and corrupt them to prove that even perfection can be destroyed...or have them killed as punishment for resistance.

# SPECIAL RULES

- Legendaries can strike Splinters without weapons and deal full damage. The use of weapons is preferred, but not obligatory.
- Paradox Legendaries share their signature moves with their classic counterparts.
- Zygarde cannot megaevolve and therefore, they cannot learn the PLZA signature move.
- Kyurem and Necrozma Variants are different Pokémon.
- Spectrier and Galastrier maintain their abilities. Cooperating with Calyrex grants them the signature move.
