# THE PARLIAMENT

Last Haven is the largest and final organized settlement to endure after the fall of the old world. To give every survivor a place suited to their typing, the land was divided into 5 main districts, adapted to the group's needs. At its heart sits the Parliament, an assembly of guardians voted to speak for and protect the needs of the type they represent.

The leader is a Silvally, chosen because they can shift between all 18 types. They act as the tie-breaker and emergency authority. They override Guild decisions only in extreme cases, and even then, they have to act accordingly to majority vote.

Each representative represents a district, a habitat zone where Pokémon of that type thrive.

### Representative Duties

- Speak for the needs, struggles, and concerns of their typing
- Mediate disputes within their district
- Protect their district with their life if needed
- Maintain communication with the Guild
- Vote on major decisions affecting Last Haven

**They are not rulers.**

They need a Noteworthy slot and high activity. They start at base BST and have immediate access to Egg and Amulet.

# GUILD RELATIONSHIP

The Guild is the heart of operations in Last Haven; the Parliament won't override its decisions unless:

- A majority vote is reached
- The decision threatens the safety or rights of a district
- The Leader invokes emergency authority (rare)

# THE TRIBUNAL OF TYPES

When an Outlaw is captured, they are brought before the entire Parliament. There's three most possible outcomes:

### Repayment

For crimes that harmed individuals or small communities. This includes labor, service, restitution and community rebuilding.

### Seclusion

For dangerous but redeemable individuals. They're confined to a monitored settlement or a cell, limited in their movements in lighter cases, rehabilitated under Guild supervision.

### Banishment

For those who cannot be trusted within Haven. They risk being banned from all settlements and the guild is authorized to detain on sight. This is the harshest punishment, essentially a social and proper death sentence.
