These roles represent high-impact positions within the Shattered Territories. Holding one means:

- **Responsibility:** You'll work closely with staff and help shape server dynamics.
- **Lore Confidence:** You must be familiar with the setting and its mechanics.
- **Activity:** Regular engagement is expected. If you disappear for months or don't respond to staff for extended periods, the role may be reassigned. Please DM Modmail if you're planning a long hiatus.

Noteworthy Characters are local celebrities (famous, infamous, or otherwise known). This role is unlimited, as long as you hold a slot, and reflects your character's popularity or notoriety in the story.

# ECHELONS

| Role | Availability |
|---|---|
| Scout Echelon | Silas |
| Builder Echelon | Cyanide |
| Provider Echelon | Xahria |
| Warden Echelon | Mayworth |
| Chronicler Echelon | Miracle |
| Void Hunter Echelon | Vesper |

Echelons are the strongest warriors of each faction, chosen for their exceptional prowess and loyalty. There is one Echelon per faction and each serves directly beneath their respective Commander.

They start at 800 BST (rewards included). Echelons are considered an elite class, and their stats threshold will be higher than those of non-Noteworthy characters.

They are expected to embody the values and strengths of their faction in both combat and narrative.

# PRAETORS & ARCHONS

| Role | Availability |
|---|---|
| Praetor/Archon | Several Positions Open |

With 30 endorsements and 3,000 BTS (Guild Senior) the character is promoted to a Praetor. Praetors are the elite cadre directly beneath an Echelon.

While Echelons are the singular champions of their faction, Praetors form the pool of potential successors who have proven themselves worthy of standing one step below the pinnacle.

Archons mirror the rank and standing of Praetors, but exist outside the guild structure (Outlaw (with reports instead of endorsements)/Independent/Splinters).

They all receive the full suite of Goal 7 rewards, though their promotion does not grant any stat increase. They have a chance to be invited to more exclusive missions.

# HIGHER SOULS

| Role | Availability |
|---|---|
| Hetzer + Weaver | Several Positions Open |
| Silhouette | Several Positions Open |
| Faraway (Cresselias only) | Several Positions Open |
| Thorn Weaver (Darkrais only) | Several Positions Open |

Higher Souls share the same stat threshold as Echelons and Commanders, but their origin is different.

These characters have been Soul Splintered by legendary entities, meaning their essence has been fractured and reshaped by direct contact with a legendary force.

Because of this, Soul Splintering must be included in the character's backstory.
